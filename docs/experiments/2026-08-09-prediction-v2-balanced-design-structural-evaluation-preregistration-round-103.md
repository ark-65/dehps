- Positive threshold: primary lower-tail numerator at most 100 of denominator
  10,000.
- Regression threshold: primary upper-tail numerator at most 100 of denominator

whether the fixed certificate is unusually low on a holdout triple-reuse

profile-matched primary comparison space. Their shared conclusion rejects a
post-generation duplicate scan as redundant and rejects any predictive or
